Plain-EnglishPrivacy

What this site stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

🎁 What we store

  • Your email address, so you can sign in. There are no passwords: we email you a one-time code.
  • Your name as it appears in each family you're part of.
  • Your wish list: gift ideas, links, prices and notes you add.
  • Family details: who's in each family, who the organisers are, exchange names, dates and budgets, no-go pairs, and the results of each draw.
  • Messages sent through the anonymous question feature.

We don't collect payment details, addresses, phone numbers, or anything else. There are no ads and no tracking or analytics.

👀 Who can see what

  • Other family members see your name. Only the person who drew you sees your wish list.
  • You see who you're buying for and their wish list. Nobody is told who drew them.
  • Your family's organisers see everyone's names and emails, and can peek at the draw if they need to (it's hidden unless they click to reveal it).
  • Other families can't see anything about your family.
  • The site owner (Jacob Hanania, who runs this site) can see each family's name, size and organisers. Because he runs the database, he could technically access all the data, and his backups include everything. He doesn't look at draws or wish lists.

🏠 Where it's kept

  • The database is hosted by Supabase in their Canada (Central) region in Montreal.
  • Sign-in codes and notification emails are sent by Resend, an email service based in the United States.
  • The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Your browser stores your sign-in session and which family you last viewed. That's it, no cookies for tracking.

🗑️ Deleting your data

  • Leave a family: sign in, open ⚙️ My account at the bottom of your page, and choose Leave this family. Your name and wish list are removed from that family.
  • Delete your account: same place, Delete my account. This removes you from every family, deletes your wish lists, and deletes your sign-in.
  • If you were part of a draw, it's reset so the organiser can draw again.
  • Backups made by the site owner may keep a copy for a while until they're replaced.

💬 Questions

Ask your family's organiser, who can pass your question on to the site owner.
