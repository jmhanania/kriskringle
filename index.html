🎁 How it works

  1. One person organises. They start your family's Kris Kringle and add everyone.
  2. Names go in the Santa hat. The draw is random, and it can keep couples from drawing each other.
  3. Everyone gets their own private page showing who they're buying for, that person's wish list, and a place to write their own.
