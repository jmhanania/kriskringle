🎁 How it works

  1. One person organises. They create an account, start your family, and add everyone's email.
  2. Everyone else signs up with that email and lands in the family automatically.
  3. Names go in the Santa hat. Everyone sees who they're buying for, that person's wish list, and a place to write their own.
